# Crocus

**A focus timer that gets out of the way.**

Crocus is a small, quiet desktop app for people who want to focus without another subscription, another account, or another thing to manage. It keeps time, takes notes, tracks tasks, plays ambient sound, and blocks the sites that pull you away — all running locally on your machine.

No cloud. No telemetry. No internet required after you download it.

## What's inside

**Focus timer** — Pomodoro cycles with configurable focus, short break, and long break durations. Focus sessions can run from 1 to 450 minutes.

**Notepad** — Write in markdown. Notes save as real `.crocusnote` files on disk, readable in any text editor.

**To-do list** — Recurring tasks (daily, weekly, monthly) that come back automatically. Click the play icon on any task to focus on it — the timer tracks what you're working on and the session log records it.

**Ambient sound** — Eight built-in color noises (white, pink, brown, red, blue, violet, grey, green). Drop your own audio files into `sounds/user/` and they appear in the sound panel.

**Website blocking** — Block the sites that pull you away, system-wide, through your Windows hosts file. Pick what to block before each focus session, or manage the list manually.

**Gentle by design** — No guilt streaks. No notifications asking why you weren't productive. The whole app is quiet, and stays quiet.
